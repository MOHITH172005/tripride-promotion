TRIPRIDE LAUNCH WEBSITE
========================

Pure HTML + CSS + JavaScript.
No Flask. No QR generation.

FILES
-----
index.html
style.css
script.js
assets/

BEFORE PUBLISHING
-----------------
Open script.js and edit:

1. launchTime
   Example:
   2026-09-23T10:00:00+05:30

2. playStoreUrl
   Replace https://example.com with the real Play Store URL.

IMPORTANT
---------
The QR should NOT contain the Play Store URL.
After deploying this website publicly, generate your QR using the
PUBLIC WEBSITE URL.

Example:
https://your-domain.com/

The same QR can then remain on your poster.


SUPPLIED ASSETS
---------------
tripride_app_qr.png       -> TripRide main app QR
tripride_rentals_qr.png   -> TripRide Rentals QR
tripride_logo.png         -> TripRide main logo
tripride_rentals_logo.jpeg -> TripRide Rentals logo

The QR images are displayed directly on the website. No QR generator is used.


REAL APP SCREENSHOTS
--------------------
8 TripRide app screenshots are included in assets/ and displayed in a horizontal
scrollable gallery on the website.


OFFICIAL WEBSITE QR
-------------------
tripride_website_qr.png -> QR for www.tripride.co.in

The website QR is shown as a third QR card and the official website
address is also displayed as a clickable link.
